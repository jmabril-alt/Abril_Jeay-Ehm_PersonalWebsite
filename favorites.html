My Favorites

A few of the things I enjoy.

01 / Food

Favorite food

My favorite food is adobo, its savory blend of soy sauce, vinegar, and garlic always feels like home, and my favorite dessert is Twix, the perfect mix of crunchy cookie, caramel, and chocolate.

02 / Music

Favorite music

"Kaganapan" by Guddisht is my favorite song because it helps me stay chill and relaxed. Its calm beat clears my mind and gives me a peaceful vibe whenever I want to take a break or unwind after a busy day.

03 / Books

Favorite book

One of my favorite books is Meditations by Marcus Aurelius, which has taught me a lot about Stoicism and how to live with calm, focus, and self-discipline. Originally written as personal reminders to himself while serving as Roman Emperor, the book offers simple, honest reflections on handling stress and staying grounded through daily challenges.

04 / Color

Favorite color

My favorite color is black because it is simple, classic, and goes well with everything. Even though some people see it as a dark color, to me it feels calm, strong, and neat.

05 / Places

Favorite place

My favorite place is our house because it is where I feel the most comfortable, relaxed, and truly myself. After a long day of classes, studying, and dealing with the busy pace outside, coming home gives me an immediate sense of peace and safety.

06 / Quote

Favorite quote

“Don't explain your philosophy. Embody it.”
